## 🚀 About Me

I'm a student developer with a strong interest in technology, science, and engineering. I enjoy taking ideas and turning them into real projects, whether that's building web applications, experimenting with electronics, or exploring the science behind our universe.

Some of the fields I'm particularly interested in:

- 🌌 **Astronomy & Astrophysics:** Exoplanets, black holes, galaxies, and space exploration.
- 🚀 **Aerospace & Space Technology:** Spacecraft, orbital mechanics, and the future of space exploration.
- 💻 **Software Engineering:** Building useful applications, learning full-stack development, and creating better digital experiences.
- 🔐 **Cybersecurity:** Windows security, system auditing, and defensive security.
- ⚡ **Electronics & Hardware:** ESP32 projects, sensors, circuits, and embedded systems.

I believe the best way to learn engineering is to actually build things, experiment, make mistakes, and understand how everything works.
